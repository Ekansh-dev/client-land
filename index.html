ABOUT DON ROHO

Emerging House DJ & Producer

DON ROHO is an emerging Tech House DJ known for delivering deep grooves, hypnotic rhythms, and high-energy underground sounds. His sets blend modern Tech House with Minimal Deep Tech influences, creating immersive dancefloor experiences.

Drawing inspiration from globally recognized artists such as Jamie Jones, Michael Bibi, and Marco Carola, DON ROHO has developed a signature sound that balances underground energy with festival-ready vibes.

As the founder of Rumours Only, he continues to build a community around authentic house music culture, connecting music lovers through memorable events and unique musical journeys.
